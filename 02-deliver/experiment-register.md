# 4D Experience Experiment Register

**Purpose:** Track candidate and active real-world experiments generated from the Business Context Framework.

The register is the single place for concrete experience opportunities.

Candidates should only move into active testing when the experience problem is meaningful, the comparison is bounded, and there is enough context to prototype safely.

---

## Candidate experiments

### BCA-EXP-001 — Customer account / duplicate context

**Opportunity**

Use richer customer + relationship + service context to improve how staff identify and handle possible duplicate accounts.

**Experience hypothesis**

> Staff given clearer contextual relationships and trust signals will make safer, faster account decisions than staff relying primarily on record-by-record comparison.

**Current experience / control**

Staff investigate and compare records through the current CRM and supporting processes.

**Possible treatment**

A prototype context view or decision aid that makes relationships, uncertainty, service context and trust signals easier to interpret.

**Test approach**

Prototype / controlled comparison.

**Status**

Candidate.

**What this could teach us about the framework**

Whether Customer Context, Relationship, Service Context and trust/stewardship signals are sufficient and useful in a real decision.

---

### BCA-EXP-002 — Parking Service Offering ↔ Knowledge

**Opportunity**

Connect Service Offering context with the reusable rules, requirements and guidance that support it.

**Experience hypothesis**

> Staff or customers presented with information organised around the Service Offering and situation will find the right answer with less searching and interpretation than through the current knowledge structure.

**Current experience / control**

Current Salesforce Knowledge / guidance journey.

**Possible treatment**

A context-driven experience organised around the Service Offering, situation and relevant business information.

**Test approach**

A/B or controlled comparison.

**Status**

Candidate.

**What this could teach us about the framework**

Whether Service Context and Information Context create a clearer and more reusable relationship than the current knowledge structure.

---

### BCA-EXP-003 — Operational change

**Opportunity**

Connect an operational event outside CRM to the affected Service Offering, information and customer interaction.

**Experience hypothesis**

> If the relevant operational context can be recognised and connected earlier, CX can provide a more timely and consistent response than when awareness depends on manual notification.

**Current experience / control**

Current manual notification and interpretation pathway.

**Possible treatment**

A context-driven alert, prompt or prototype experience that connects the operational change to the affected Service Offering and customer interaction context.

**Test approach**

Pilot / controlled comparison.

**Status**

Candidate.

**What this could teach us about the framework**

Whether Operational Context can meaningfully connect changes outside CRM to Service, Information and Interaction Context.

## Candidate selection rule

Choose the next experiment based on:

- meaningful customer or staff friction;
- ability to create a bounded comparison;
- access to enough context to prototype;
- an observable measure;
- low enough risk to test safely.

Do not choose an experiment merely because the technology is easy.

---

## Experiment pattern

For each experiment capture:

1. **Opportunity** — what experience are we trying to improve?
2. **Hypothesis** — what do we believe the framework makes possible?
3. **Control/current experience** — what happens today?
4. **Treatment/prototype** — what changes when Business Context is applied?
5. **Measure** — what observable difference matters?
6. **Result** — what happened?
7. **Framework implication** — what should we keep, change or remove?

## Delivery principle

4D is where the framework earns credibility.

A positive result strengthens the model.

A negative or ambiguous result is equally useful if it exposes a weak assumption, unnecessary concept or missing relationship.
