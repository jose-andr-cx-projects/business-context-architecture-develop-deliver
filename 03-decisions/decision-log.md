## BCA-DEC-003 — Develop top-down

**Status:** Agreed  
**Date:** 6 October 2026

3D will define a Business Context Framework from a strong business and customer point of view rather than deriving the future model bottom-up from current systems and processes.

Current reality must be able to map into the framework, but it does not define the framework.

## BCA-DEC-004 — Proposed protected expert war-room

**Status:** Working proposal — implementation decision pending  
**Date:** 6 October 2026 (original direction); 22 October 2026 (status clarified)

The original working proposal is for a small, selected group to develop the framework, drawing on relevant expertise without requiring organisation-wide representation or consensus before testing. Inherited process and system assumptions should be challenged explicitly.

**Decision boundary:** The original 'Agreed' status represented agreement to the working design direction. It must **not** be read as Dennis's or CX leadership's approval of the implementation model, membership, resource allocation, engagement approach, or permission to convene the group. See BCA-DEC-008.

## BCA-DEC-005 — Evidence over consensus

**Status:** Agreed  
**Date:** 6 October 2026

The framework may proceed on explicit assumptions where evidence is incomplete.

Real-world evidence from 4D has authority to challenge or overturn the model.

## BCA-DEC-006 — Deliver through experience experiments

**Status:** Agreed  
**Date:** 6 October 2026

4D will apply the Business Context Framework to real opportunities and test whether it creates better customer or staff experiences.

A/B tests are preferred where practical; controlled comparisons, prototypes or pilots are valid alternatives.

4D is not an organisation-wide architecture implementation.

## BCA-DEC-007 — Earn adoption

**Status:** Agreed  
**Date:** 6 October 2026

The Business Context Framework should earn wider adoption by demonstrating value through use.

It should not depend on organisation-wide agreement before useful testing begins.

## BCA-DEC-008 — Decide how to implement 3D Develop after Dennis's feedback

**Status:** Pending — no implementation go-ahead  
**Recorded:** 22 October 2026  
**Decision sought from:** Dennis / appropriate CX leadership (formal decision authority and sponsor involvement to be confirmed)  
**Scope:** The **operating and engagement approach** for developing the Business Context Framework, not approval of any technology architecture.

### What was proposed

3D Develop would use a protected, time-boxed expert 'war-room' to create a coherent, top-down Business Context Framework. 4D Deliver would apply the model to bounded customer/staff experience tests and use evidence to refine it. This is a proposal, not an authorised working group.

### Dennis's feedback — separated from interpretation

**Directly expressed in the José–Dennis project-roadmap discussion:**

- Dennis said he **agreed with the broader shift** away from a narrow, CRM-centred improvement lens.
- He identified a missing **connection/orchestration from information and platforms to the customer experience**. The ability to bring data together for analysis is valuable but does not, on its own, deliver a joined-up customer interaction.
- He wanted the **right audience** for the strategic discussion, rather than defaulting to those dealing with day-to-day account fixes, and warned that continual diversion of capacity into historic problems could stall progress.
- He raised the need to **time-box and align capability or investment conversations to business-planning milestones**, suggesting that even a concise one-page articulation of the gap could help progress the discussion.
- He emphasised exploring what data actually tells us and **creating and testing more ambitious customer experiences**, with privacy and data limitations examined rather than assumed away.
- Data 360, Qualtrics and Marketing Cloud were raised as **examples for examining orchestration capability**, **not selected solutions**.

**Related feedback as reported from subsequent CAM discussions:**

- Dennis wants relevant people **brought along during development**, with an opportunity to contribute and understand the direction, rather than being presented with a completed answer.
- This does **not** establish who has decision authority or whether every participant must co-design the framework.

**Interpretation for 3D/4D — not a decision by Dennis:**

The challenge is to retain independent, principle-led synthesis without creating a closed design exercise that lacks the right input, sponsorship or implementation pathway. A protected expert phase *could* coexist with purposeful stakeholder checkpoints, explicit decisions and bounded experience validation; that accommodation has **not** been approved.

### Decision required

**Do we proceed with the proposed protected war-room, modify how it engages and operates, or hold 3D commencement until priorities and authority are resolved?**

Options for leadership consideration (none selected):

1. **Proceed with conditions:** a bounded expert design phase, named accountable lead, targeted operational/digital/data input at defined points, clear decision gates and a candidate 4D proof.
2. **Revise the operating approach:** agree a different way to create and validate v0.1 while preserving business-led definitions and evidence-based testing.
3. **Defer commencement:** settle sponsorship, priority, decision rights or capacity before initiating 3D.

### What must be resolved before a go-ahead

- The decision owner/sponsor, strategic audience and accountable lead.
- Core expertise versus stakeholders who should contribute or validate at defined points; how operational staff are brought along without returning to indefinite consensus-seeking.
- The smallest time-boxed deliverable, intended customer/staff experience opportunity, and what constitutes enough evidence to proceed.
- Alignment with existing CX transformation, account-management and channel priorities; how to protect capacity from routine fixes.
- Whether a one-page capability/experience gap statement is needed for a business-planning decision.
- When technical orchestration needs assessment, without predetermining Data 360, Databricks, Salesforce or another platform.

### Current disposition / next action

**Remain at the decision point.** Do **not** schedule the war-room, appoint participants, treat a technology option as selected, or promote the framework to an approved standard on the basis of the current proposal.

At the next leadership discussion, record **proceed / revise / defer**, the authorised decision-maker, any conditions, accountable owner and only then the next bounded activity in this log.
